## 1. Purpose and Non-Resolution Rule

Record all pending institutional business decisions (`LAC-01` through `LAC-13`), explicitly highlighting the five foundational decisions mandated by C-01 acceptance criterion 8 (`LAC-01`, `LAC-02`, `LAC-04`, `LAC-07`, `LAC-11`), together with who decides each item, the target deadline/milestone before which the decision is required, and the mandatory technical behavior while the decision remains pending.

> **Mandatory invariant (`RES-06` / C-01 item 8):** None of these institutional decisions is silently resolved by technical contracts, database schemas, seed data, or application code. While a decision is marked `PENDING`, modules must enforce the documented pending-state behavior (parameterization without default values, explicit pending states, or feature/field blocking). Technical validation and integrity controls (`VAL-01`..`VAL-12`, `OP-01`..`OP-09`, mandatory unique CPF) are already approved engineering requirements and are never waived by a pending `LAC`.

## 4. Procedure for Recording a Resolved Institutional Decision

When the institutional representative formally resolves any `LAC-*` item:

1. Update the item's status in this file from `PENDING` to `APPROVED` (or `PARTIALLY APPROVED`), recording:
   - Decision summary and exact parameters;
   - Name and role of the institutional decision-maker;
   - Date of decision and link/reference to the written evidence (meeting minutes, signed document, or issue comment);
   - Affected requirement IDs (`RF-*`, `RN-*`, `AC-*`) and ADRs updated.
2. Obtain technical review from Fabricio (`@Fabricio-Fontenele`) and Gabriel (lead) (`@Gabriel-afk-9`) confirming that code/configuration changes match the recorded decision without exceeding its scope.
